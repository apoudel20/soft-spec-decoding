# Branching and superposed-future architectures (parallel branches, latent superposition, tree attention, co-trained drafters)

Scope note. Read depth is marked per item. "abstract" means only the arXiv abstract page or a search snippet of it was read. "secondary" means a blog, conference listing or aggregator. "memory" means stated from prior knowledge and not re-verified this session, so the writer should treat it as unconfirmed. Exactness labels: EXACT means output distribution is provably identical to the base model's sequential decoding. APPROX means the output changes (new model behaviour, different text). None of the parallel-reasoning methods in section 1 are exact. Only the speculative methods in section 4 are exact.

## 1. Parallel-branch generation inside one model (forking and merging the KV cache)

### Takeaway
A 2025-2026 line of work (APR, Hogwild!, Multiverse, PASTA, ThreadWeaver, NPR, Parallel-R1, ParaThinker, Multiplex-style variants) trains or prompts one model to fork its own generation into branches that share a prefix KV cache and later merge. They report 1.2x to 4.6x latency reductions with equal or better accuracy, but all of them are APPROXIMATE. They change what the model outputs, so none is a lossless acceleration of a fixed model.

### Cited Findings
- APR, Learning Adaptive Parallel Reasoning (arXiv 2504.15466, Pan, Li, Lian et al., Berkeley). The model emits spawn() and join() to launch child threads, and parent and child threads are trained end to end with RL with no predefined structure. On Countdown it reaches 83.4% vs 60.0% within a 4k context, 80.1% vs 66.6% at 20k total tokens, and 75.2% vs 57.3% at about 5,000 ms latency. APPROX. (abstract) - [arXiv 2504.15466](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15466)
- Hogwild! Inference (arXiv 2504.06261, Rodionov, Garipov ... Alistarh, NeurIPS 2025). Several instances of the same LLM run in parallel over a shared, concurrently updated KV cache. Each worker's KV blocks are stitched into the others' views in different orders by adjusting RoPE, rotating only the current queries rather than re-rotating cached keys, so no recomputation is needed. QwQ and DeepSeek-R1 use the shared cache with no fine-tuning, making plans, flagging each other's errors and reusing each other's observations. No verified speedup numbers were found. APPROX, training-free. (abstract + search snippet) - [arXiv 2504.06261](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.06261); [NeurIPS 2025 poster](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/poster/115744); code at github.com/eqimp/hogwild_llm
- Multiverse (arXiv 2506.09991, Yang, An, Liu, Tianqi Chen, Beidi Chen, NeurIPS 2025). A MapReduce paradigm is built into generation with Map (decompose), Process (parallel branches) and Reduce (merge) stages. Data is Multiverse-1K, sequential chains rewritten by an LLM into parallel structure. Training uses a mask that blocks sibling-branch attention while staying causal-attention compatible. Serving is an SGLang extension where branches share the prefix via RadixAttention and the join copies branch KV caches and edits the page table to stitch non-contiguous blocks. Multiverse-32B (Qwen2.5-32B base, SFT) outperforms AR LLMs by 1.87% on average at equal context length and gives up to 2x wall-clock speedup per generated token. Data, weights, engine and recipes are open. APPROX. (search snippet + secondary) - [arXiv 2506.09991](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09991v2); [NeurIPS listing](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/poster/115810); [BAIR blog](https://bair.berkeley.edu/blog/2026/05/08/adaptive-parallel-reasoning/)
- ThreadWeaver (arXiv 2512.07843, Lian et al., Meta Superintelligence Labs, Berkeley, UCSF, ICML 2026 oral). A two-stage data pipeline (GPT-5 rewriting identifies parallel blocks in sequential traces) feeds a trie-based design that runs on unmodified inference engines. The client concatenates branch text and the engine re-prefills it for the conclusion. Training flattens a prefix tree with an ancestor-only attention mask. RL reward is 1 - L_critical/L_total, gated on correctness. On Qwen3-8B it gets 79.9% on AIME24 and up to 1.53x average token-latency speedup across six math benchmarks, matching sequential accuracy. APPROX. (search snippet + secondary) - [arXiv 2512.07843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07843?context=cs); [project page](https://threadweaver-parallel.github.io); [BAIR blog](https://bair.berkeley.edu/blog/2026/05/08/adaptive-parallel-reasoning/)
- Native Parallel Reasoner, NPR (arXiv 2512.07461, Wu et al., ICML 2026). It is teacher-free, self-distilled RL that stitches thread KV caches like Multiverse and treats sequential fallback as failure, targeting a 100% "Genuine Parallelism Rate". On Qwen3-4B (Instruct-2507 and non-thinking) it reports up to 24.5% accuracy gains and up to 4.6x speedup across eight benchmarks (AIME25 4.6x, HMMT25 4.1x, AMC23 2.9x), so gains are larger on harder problems. An aggregator claims 76.7% on AIME25 vs 72.8% for Multiverse-32B, which is unverified in the primary text. A v3 from May 2026 exists and numbers may differ. APPROX. (abstract) - [arXiv 2512.07461](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07461); [hyper.ai summary](https://hyper.ai/en/papers/2512.07461)
- Parallel-R1 (arXiv 2509.07980, Zheng et al.). Parallel structure is used as an RL scaffold. Use of parallel structure rose from 13.6% to 63% with little accuracy change, and the model reverted to sequential after step 200 when the parallel reward was removed. This suggests models do not intrinsically prefer branching. (secondary, BAIR blog) - [BAIR blog](https://bair.berkeley.edu/blog/2026/05/08/adaptive-parallel-reasoning/)
- ParaThinker (arXiv 2509.04475, Wen et al.). There are two fixed stages, parallel thoughts then a summary, using `<think_i>` tokens, thought-specific positional embeddings and a two-phase attention mask. This is directly relevant to branch-aware positional encoding. GroupThink (arXiv 2505.11107) runs interdependent threads that see each other's partial output at token granularity. (secondary) - [BAIR blog](https://bair.berkeley.edu/blog/2026/05/08/adaptive-parallel-reasoning/)
- PASTA, Learning to Keep a Promise (arXiv 2502.11517, Tian Jin et al., MIT and Google, ICML 2025). The model annotates semantically independent spans with PASTA-LANG tags, and an interpreter launches parallel decoding threads and synchronizes at sync tags. Training is SFT on annotated SlimOrca followed by preference optimization over quality and latency. On AlpacaEval it Pareto-dominates prior async decoding (Skeleton-of-Thought, APAR), with 1.2x to 1.9x speedup and length-controlled win-rate changes of +2.2% to -7.1%. The authors warn that arithmetic averaging of speedups inflates figures. APPROX. (abstract + snippet) - [arXiv 2502.11517](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11517v2); [ICML 2025](https://icml.cc/virtual/2025/poster/44837)
- Self-Orchestrating Language Models (arXiv 2609.14850, Tian Jin, Sep 2026) frames PASTA, TIP (evicting intermediate reasoning from the KV cache) and Planned Diffusion (an AR plan sets the order in which chunks are denoised in parallel) as one idea, where the model annotates semantic dependence and the runtime picks a strategy. No numbers in the abstract. (abstract) - [arXiv 2609.14850](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14850)
- Multi-Stream LLMs (arXiv 2605.12460, Su, Yang, Li, Geiping, May 2026). Instruction-tunes models with several parallel input and output streams per forward pass, one stream per role, all causally dependent on earlier timesteps. It claims efficiency gains but the abstract gives no quantified speedup. (abstract) - [arXiv 2605.12460](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12460)
- Skeleton-of-Thought (arXiv 2307.15337, Ning et al., ICLR 2024). The model is prompted to write an outline, then expands points in parallel (batched or API-parallel). It is a heuristic and training-free. I recall a reported speedup of up to about 2.39x, but this is from memory and was not verified this session. APPROX. - [BAIR blog listing](https://bair.berkeley.edu/blog/2026/05/08/adaptive-parallel-reasoning/)
- A survey of parallel reasoning (arXiv 2510.12164) defines the field as making multiple attempts in parallel before answering and then aggregating them. PaCoRe (arXiv 2601.05593) runs many parallel trajectories per round and compacts findings into context between rounds. (search snippet) - [Survey](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12164v1); [PaCoRe](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2601.05593)

### Inferences
- There are two implementation families for branched KV caches. (a) Physical stitching (Multiverse, NPR, Hogwild!) edits page tables or RoPE so the merge step attends over all branch KV without recomputing. (b) Engine-agnostic re-prefill (ThreadWeaver) concatenates branch text and prefills it again. Family (a) is the closest prior art for a "branched KV cache rather than linear" design.
- Training masks are consistently "attend to ancestors only, siblings blocked" (Multiverse, ThreadWeaver). This is the same tree mask Medusa and EAGLE use for verification (section 3), so a target trained on such masks could in principle verify a drafter token tree and run its own branch tree with one kernel.
- Speedups in this family come from shortening the critical path (latency), not from cheaper total compute. They are not lossless, so they are complementary to, not substitutes for, exact speculative decoding.

### Gaps
- Verified Hogwild! speedup numbers and the per-benchmark accuracy tables for Multiverse were not read (only abstracts and snippets).
- I found no paper combining in-model branching (Multiverse or NPR style) with exact speculative verification of each branch. This appears unexplored.
- Skeleton-of-Thought numbers are from memory.

## 2. Superposition of futures in latent space (continuous thoughts, soft tokens)

### Takeaway
Theory shows continuous thoughts can hold a superposition of search frontiers (Coconut BFS, Reasoning by Superposition), and training-free soft-token methods (Soft Thinking, Mixture of Inputs) give modest gains. But a careful analysis (Single-threaded Reasoners, ICLR 2026) finds that pretrained LLMs fed soft-token mixtures mostly follow the top token, behaving like greedy decoding. Real multi-path exploration seems to need training (Soft Tokens Hard Truths, Multiplex Thinking) or injected noise. Parallel Reconsideration (2611.05724) exists but is a speculative-decoding drafter method, not a latent-superposition method.

### Cited Findings
- Coconut (arXiv 2412.06769, Hao, Sukhbaatar, Su, Li, Hu, Weston, Tian, Meta, COLM 2025). The last hidden state is fed back as the next input embedding (a "continuous thought"). The abstract claims continuous thoughts encode multiple alternative next steps, allowing BFS-like search instead of committing to one path, and outperform CoT on logical reasoning needing planning. (abstract. From memory, the tasks are ProsQA, ProntoQA and GSM8K on GPT-2, unverified) - [arXiv 2412.06769](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06769)
- Reasoning by Superposition (arXiv 2505.12514, Zhu, Hao et al., NeurIPS 2025). A two-layer transformer with continuous CoT solves directed graph reachability in steps proportional to the graph diameter D, vs O(n^2) for the best known constant-depth discrete-CoT construction. Each continuous thought is a superposition of multiple search frontiers (parallel BFS), while discrete CoT must sample one path. The superposition emerges in training even with only optimal-path supervision. A strict lower bound for discrete CoT is left open. (search snippet of abstract) - [arXiv 2505.12514](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12514); [NeurIPS 2025](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/72c363c2a573ca2128bd176d3317696b-Abstract-Conference.html)
- Emergence of Superposition (arXiv 2509.23365) analyses the training dynamics behind this on the same task, in two stages (thought generation, answer prediction). (snippet) - [arXiv 2509.23365](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2509.23365)
- Soft Thinking (arXiv 2505.15778, Zhang, He, ... Xin Eric Wang). It is training-free. Each step feeds a "concept token", the probability-weighted mixture of token embeddings, claimed to implicitly explore multiple paths. It reports up to +2.48 pass@1 and up to 22.4% fewer tokens than CoT on math and code. APPROX. (abstract) - [arXiv 2505.15778](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15778)
- Evidence against. LLMs are Single-threaded Reasoners (arXiv 2508.03440, Wu et al., Baidu, ICLR 2026). Vanilla Soft Thinking outputs are highly similar (ROUGE-L) to greedy decoding, because models mostly follow the top token in the soft input (the "Greedy Pitfall"). It does not beat discrete sampling. Stochastic Soft Thinking with Gumbel-Softmax randomness alleviates this. Tested on QwQ-32B, Skywork-OR1-32B and others over eight benchmarks. (abstract + snippet) - [arXiv 2508.03440](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.03440v4); [ICLR 2026](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2026/poster/10011037)
- Mixture of Inputs (arXiv 2505.14827, Zhuang, Liu, Singh, Shang, Gao, NeurIPS 2025). The input is a Bayesian posterior blend of the sampled one-hot token (observation) and the full distribution (prior). It is training-free with negligible overhead and consistently improves math, code and GPQA on QwQ-32B, Nemotron-Super-49B, Gemma-3-27B and DAPO-Qwen-32B. Because it keeps the sampled token as the anchor, it is a softened single path rather than true multi-branching. APPROX. (snippet) - [arXiv 2505.14827](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14827)
- Soft Tokens, Hard Truths (arXiv 2509.19170, Butt, Kwiatkowski, Labiad, Kempe, Ollivier, ICLR 2026). RL with soft tokens (token mixtures plus input-embedding noise) learns continuous CoTs of hundreds of tokens. Llama and Qwen up to 8B match discrete CoT at pass@1 and beat it at pass@32 (more diverse). The best recipe is to train continuous and decode discrete, and it better preserves out-of-domain behaviour. (snippet) - [arXiv 2509.19170](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19170)
- Multiplex Thinking (arXiv 2601.08808, Tang, Dong, Hao, Dong, Wei, Gu, Microsoft, Jan 2026). It samples K candidate tokens per step and merges their embeddings into one "multiplex token", which is near-discrete when confident and multi-path when uncertain, without lengthening the sequence. It is trained with on-policy RL and beats discrete CoT and RL baselines from Pass@1 to Pass@1024 with shorter outputs. The abstract gives no numbers or models. This is the closest existing "token-wise branch-and-merge in latent space". (abstract) - [arXiv 2601.08808](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08808)
- Parallel Reconsideration, verified to exist. "Give the Drafter a Second Chance to Guess: Parallel Reconsideration for Speculative Decoding" (arXiv 2611.05724, Le, Dong, Khadem, Sun, Song, Huang, Radpour, Shrivastava, He, submitted 3 Nov 2026). A feature-level drafter uses its own uncertainty to flag hesitant draft tokens and reconsiders them in extra passes run in parallel with the main draft pass. A tailored training recipe adds the refined guesses to the draft tree as extra candidates. Acceptance length improves 5.3% to 10.2% and speedup 3.5% to 9.2% on average over baselines. EXACT, since it only changes the draft tree and verification is standard. (abstract) - [arXiv 2611.05724](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05724)

### Inferences
- The evidence on "feed distributions to attention to explore several branches at once" is mixed and depends on training. Theory and toy-task training (Coconut, Superposition) support it. Training-free soft inputs to pretrained LLMs mostly collapse to the argmax path (Single-threaded Reasoners), so soft inputs alone should not be expected to make an off-the-shelf target hold several futures. RL-trained mixtures (Soft Tokens Hard Truths, Multiplex) recover diversity, mostly visible at large pass@k.
- For a "target-side future gist tree", this suggests the target must be trained to emit a branchable latent. A training-free soft-token gist from a frozen target will likely encode mainly the top continuation.
- Parallel Reconsideration shows that uncertainty-gated extra drafter passes, which add width only where the drafter hesitates, give about 4% to 9% extra speedup on top of tree drafters. This is a concrete baseline for a drafter-side token tree.

### Gaps
- No paper found that uses soft or superposed tokens inside speculative decoding drafts (for example a drafter conditioned on a target superposition latent). This appears open.
- Coconut's quantitative results and models were not verified this session.

## 3. Tree-structured or non-linear attention and state

### Takeaway
Tree attention masks with ancestor-only visibility and depth-based position IDs are standard for verification (Medusa, EAGLE) and are now used for training on trees of continuations (Tree Training, AReaL-DTA, ThreadWeaver, Multiverse). Branch-specific positional embeddings appear in ParaThinker and RoPE re-stitching in Hogwild!. I found no dedicated work on forking SSM or recurrent state for generation beyond Tree Training's SSM adaptation.

### Cited Findings
- Medusa (arXiv 2401.10774) uses a tree attention mask where each candidate token attends only to its ancestors, with position indices set by tree depth, to verify many candidate continuations in one forward pass. EXACT under rejection-sampling verification. (snippet) - [arXiv 2401.10774](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.10774)
- Tree Training (arXiv 2511.00413). Agentic rollouts that share prefixes are serialized depth-first so each token is processed once, with full-attention and state-space layers adapted so log-probs match per-branch computation. It reports up to 6.2x end-to-end SFT and RL speedup (v2 said 3.9x, so the figures differ across versions). This is the one found case of SSM state handled over a tree. (snippet) - [arXiv 2511.00413](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.00413v4)
- AReaL-DTA (arXiv 2602.00482) avoids materialising a full tree attention mask (which scales poorly) by depth-first traversal of the prefix tree, computing each node once and aggregating gradients across continuations, for RL post-training. (snippet) - [arXiv 2602.00482](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.00482)
- ThreadWeaver flattens a prefix tree with an ancestor-only mask for training. Multiverse blocks sibling attention. ParaThinker uses thought-specific positional embeddings plus a two-phase mask. Hogwild! re-stitches KV blocks by rotating queries under RoPE. (secondary) - [BAIR blog](https://bair.berkeley.edu/blog/2026/05/08/adaptive-parallel-reasoning/)

### Inferences
- A model trained on trees of continuations with an ancestor-only mask is already the norm in 2025-2026 RL infra (Tree Training, AReaL-DTA). The infrastructure cost of training a target on branched futures is therefore solved. What is missing is an objective that makes the target expose those branches as a latent for a drafter.
- Position-ID choice matters. Medusa and EAGLE use depth positions (siblings share positions). ParaThinker adds per-branch embeddings. Hogwild! shows RoPE lets branch blocks be re-ordered at merge time without recomputation.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 "tree transformer for generation" with a fundamentally non-linear positional scheme was found within the search budget.
- No work found on forking Mamba or RWKV state for speculative or parallel generation, other than Tree Training's training-time adaptation.

## 4. Co-trained or co-adaptive drafter and target (target exposes a branchable latent)

### Takeaway
Exact methods where the target itself is modified to help drafting exist in three forms. (a) MTP heads trained with the target (DeepSeek-V3, GLM-5). (b) Fine-tuning the target with mask tokens and a sampler head (Apple 2507.11851). (c) Shared-trunk self-speculation (LayerSkip). Separately, 2026 work co-trains drafters online during target RL. I found no work that trains the target to emit an explicit tree or gist latent for the drafter, which is the gap the user's idea targets.

### Cited Findings
- Your LLM Knows the Future (arXiv 2507.11851, Samragh, Kundu ... Farajtabar, Apple, Jul 2025). A pretrained model given redundant pad tokens already ranks the correct next tokens within the top 200 logits. Fine-tuning the target with `<mask>` tokens (gated LoRA keeps the base behaviour) pushes them into the top 10, and a lightweight sampler head refines further. On Tulu3-8B predicting 8 extra tokens it is nearly 5x faster on code and math and about 2.5x on chat and knowledge, with no quality loss. EXACT, since outputs are verified (the paper uses speculative-style verification). (abstract + Apple page + press) - [arXiv 2507.11851](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11851v1); [Apple ML Research](https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/prediction-potential); [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/08/08/apple-research-teaches-llms-to-think-faster/)
- DeepSeek-V3 MTP (arXiv 2412.19437). One MTP module is trained jointly with the main model during pretraining and reused at inference to predict 2 tokens. Second-token acceptance is 85% to 90% across topics, giving 1.8x TPS (internal measurement, hardware not described). EXACT when used as a speculative draft. - [DeepSeek-V3 report](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.19437)
- GLM-5 (arXiv 2602.15763) reports that a training-inference discrepancy lowers second-token acceptance and so shares parameters across MTP layers. (snippet) - [GLM-5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.15763)
- LayerSkip (arXiv 2404.16710, Elhoushi et al., Meta, ACL 2024). The model is trained with layer dropout and an early-exit loss with one shared LM head. It drafts with early layers and verifies with the rest of the same network, sharing compute, activations and KV. It reports up to 2.16x on CNN/DM, 1.82x on coding and 2.0x on TOPv2. EXACT. (snippet) - [arXiv 2404.16710](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16710)
- Online Draft Co-Training (arXiv 2609.07108). The drafter is co-trained during large-scale long-context RL post-training. Co-trained runs track baseline reward on all six targets, with rollout throughput above baseline throughout. The target is not changed to help the drafter. (snippet, unverified date) - [arXiv 2609.07108](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.07108)
- Draft in Parallel, Condition Through Depth (arXiv 2609.36173). Jointly trains drafter candidate generation and predecessor-conditioned decoding under one loss with the target frozen. (snippet) - [arXiv 2609.36173](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.36173)
- Cross-Attention Speculative Decoding (arXiv 2505.24544) is a cross-attention drafter over target states, competitive with EAGLE-2 and more training-efficient. It notes self-speculative methods avoid co-training cost but give weaker speedups. (snippet) - [arXiv 2505.24544](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.24544)
- Infra. SpecForge (arXiv 2603.18567) separates target and draft parallelism for EAGLE-3 training. TorchSpec (PyTorch blog) compares co-located vs offline hidden-state training and reports a Kimi K2.5 EAGLE-3 draft. (snippet) - [SpecForge](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18567); [PyTorch blog](https://pytorch.org/?p=59129)

### Inferences
- Apple 2507.11851 is the clearest evidence that a target can be cheaply fine-tuned (gated LoRA) to expose future information to a drafter without changing its own outputs. A "future gist tree" latent is a natural generalisation from a linear mask-token future to a branched one.
- DeepSeek and GLM MTP show that joint pretraining of target plus draft head is production practice, but the head predicts a single linear future. No branched MTP was found.
- The combination "target trained with ancestor-only tree masks (section 3) plus target-exposed future latent (Apple, MTP) plus drafter token tree verified exactly (Medusa, EAGLE, Parallel Reconsideration)" was not found in any single paper.

### Gaps
- No primary source found for "symbiotic" speculative decoding where the target loss includes a drafter-acceptance term. Searched with generic queries only.
- Exact Apple architecture details (gated LoRA, verification procedure) come from my knowledge of the paper and the Apple page summary, not a full read.
- Dates and venues for the 2603, 2609 and 2611 preprints were taken from arXiv abstract pages or snippets only.
