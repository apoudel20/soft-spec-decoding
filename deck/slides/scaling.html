Tripling the data and roughly doubling the model gives 3 to 7%. Conditioning on the target’s states at 56 earlier positions gives +5% on math (best of 16) and −1 to −2% on code and chat. Neither closes much of the gap to the oracle.

Not controlled comparisons. The 285M model was trained from scratch for 24k steps. The 158M models were warm-started from the deterministic model. The context run used batch 64 instead of 128, 20k steps, and context from layers 30 and 36 only. Training data is Qwen3-8B’s own non-thinking answers (PerfectBlend regeneration). It has no overlap with MT-Bench, GSM8K test, HumanEval, MBPP validation or UltraChat test. 1 of 40 GSM8K-train prompts used as the math dev set shares its first 120 characters with a training prompt.
