Not controlled comparisons. The 285M model was trained from scratch and the 158M models were warm-started. The context run used batch 64. Training data is Qwen3-8B’s own non-thinking answers (PerfectBlend regeneration), with no overlap with MT-Bench, GSM8K test, HumanEval, MBPP validation or UltraChat test.
