Gister’s chain and DFlash cost the same per round; DFlash wins on accepted tokens. Trees raise acceptance 18 to 32% but cost 45 to 55% more per round here, because sampling latents and verifying 60 to 120 nodes is not free with 4-bit kernels.

Qwen3-8B 4-bit, RTX 4070 Ti, nothing else on the GPU (plain decoding 22 tok/s), greedy, 20 prompts per set, 256 tokens, one loop for every drafter, verified to match DFlash’s own decoder.
