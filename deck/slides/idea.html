1 · METHOD

A two-stage drafter over the target’s hidden states

Stage 1: autoencoder

Encodes the target’s hidden states at 8 consecutive positions (layers 18, 24, 30, 36) into a 2,048-d latent z. The decoder output goes through the target’s frozen LM head.

Stage 2: flow sampler

Samples the next latent zt+1 given zt and the target’s hidden states at the current positions. Rectified flow, 2 Euler steps.

Verification

K samples decode to K drafts of 8 tokens. Shared prefixes are merged into a tree, verified in one forward pass of the target.

Related work

ChainFlow (VAE over target states, one deterministic 8-position draft). FlashMap (flow over multi-layer target states, several draws as a tree). MFD and DBLast (multi-future draft trees). CALM (one latent per K-token chunk). DFlash and DDTree (block diffusion drafter, per-position top-k trees). Each component appears in prior work. Gister combines sampled latents of the target’s own future states with joint decoding of each sample.

The results so far test whether sampling in this latent space helps drafting. They do not show an improvement over these methods.
