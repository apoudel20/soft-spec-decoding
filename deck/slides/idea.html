01 · THE IDEA

Sample several futures of the target’s own states, check them at once

1 · Compress

Stage 1 turns the target’s hidden states for 8 tokens into one 2,048-dim latent and decodes them back through the target’s frozen output layer.

2 · Sample futures

Stage 2, a flow-matching sampler, draws several possible next latents from the current one plus the target’s raw states.

3 · Verify as a tree

Each sample decodes to a whole 8-token continuation. Shared prefixes merge into one tree, checked in one target pass. Output stays the target’s.

Closest prior work

ChainFlow (VAE over target states, flow to 8 positions, one deterministic draft) · FlashMap (flow from multi-layer states, several draws as a tree) · MFD, DBLast (coherent multi-future trees) · CALM (one latent per token chunk) · DFlash + DDTree (per-slot trees). Gister’s specific combination: several sampled latents of a chunk of the target’s own future states, each decoded jointly.

Be explicit that the components exist in prior work. The contribution is the combination, and the evidence so far is about whether sampling in this latent space works, not about beating these methods.
