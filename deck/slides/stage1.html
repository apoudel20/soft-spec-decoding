Limitations

We required 99.5 / 99.5 / 99.0% top-1 and a total variation of 0.005 to 0.01. The autoencoder misses both (total variation 0.06 / 0.06 / 0.20), so all results are for greedy decoding. Reaching these numbers required giving the encoder the LM-head row of each position’s top-1 token. In an earlier z = 1,024 ablation without that input, the last layer alone gives 93.7 / 92.3 / 76.8% against 94.0 / 92.6 / 77.6% for all four layers. The latent mostly encodes the 8 tokens.
