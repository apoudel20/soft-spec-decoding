9 · PROPOSED WORK

A longer-horizon latent

The current latent is trained to reconstruct 8 tokens exactly, and it mostly encodes those tokens. We propose a latent that summarizes a longer future and is trained by prediction in latent space.

Approach

· Encode the target’s hidden states over the next 64 to 256 tokens into z.

· Train with a latent prediction loss (JEPA-style, EMA target encoder, variance regularization) in place of token reconstruction.

· Sample z with flow matching. Each branch of the draft tree comes from a different sample.

· A decoder conditioned on z and the context drafts the next 8 to 16 tokens, which the target verifies.

1 · Oracle z

Train the decoder with z from the true future and compare with the same decoder without z. If acceptance does not improve, a predicted z will not help either.

2 · Predicted z

Replace the oracle with stage-2 samples and measure the gap.

3 · Trees

Compare with DFlash and DDTree per-position trees at equal node count.

4 · Scale

100M+ positions of Qwen3-8B outputs and a 0.5B to 1B stage 2. On this GPU, 800M positions is about 3 days of target forward passes.

Earlier experiments (RESULTS.md) added a single averaged future vector to a frozen DFlash and saw no gain, even with the true future. They do not test a decoder trained with z from the start. Risks: the oracle may still not help, and latent prediction objectives can collapse without regularization.

Test 1 takes a few days and determines whether tests 2 to 4 are worth running.
