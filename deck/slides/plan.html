09 · THE PLAN AND THE ASK

What the compute would test, in order

1 · Full context

Cross-attend to the target’s states at every earlier position, the structural limit we measured.

2 · Matched budget

Sampled latent tree vs DFlash / DDTree per-slot tree at the same node count.

3 · Is the latent needed

At small scale the latent edges direct token prediction by ~3% (2.80 vs 2.73 math). Re-test at scale.

4 · Data

100M+ on-policy positions. Here, 800M positions is about 3 days of target forward passes.

5 · Model

A warm-started 0.5B to 1B sampler; 1B barely fits this 12 GB GPU.

6 · Latency

One-step sampler, CUDA graphs, a bf16 target, so a tree drafts near the chain’s 11 ms.

Risks: trees may not pay off even with sharper samples; stage 1 must be tightened before sampling at temperature above 0. Deliverables: result files, training dashboards, checkpoints, a weekly write-up.
