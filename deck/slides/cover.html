SPECULATIVE DECODING · QWEN3-8B (4-BIT)

Gister: speculative drafting
from sampled future latents

We train a generative model over Qwen3-8B’s future hidden states and use its samples as draft continuations, which the target then verifies with the same greedy rule as DFlash. Results are from a single RTX 4070 Ti. The last slide proposes what to run with more compute.

Greedy decoding throughout. With the 4-bit target, near-ties can resolve differently in batched verification, so outputs match target-only decoding on 4 to 18 of 20 prompts per set (DFlash: 4 to 15). All numbers are in results/ at github.com/apoudel20/soft-spec-decoding.

Status: sampling in the latent space raises acceptance, but the drafter accepts fewer tokens than DFlash, and trees do not yet pay for their cost on this GPU.
